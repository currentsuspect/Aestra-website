11 PRs. Takes landed — record until you get it, sort it out after.

- **new**: Multi-take recording. Keep every pass, switch between them cleanly, and nothing gets overwritten while you're chasing the good one.
- **new**: CLAP plugins now expose their parameters properly, including the ones that don't ship a parameter list at all.
- **fix**: A batch of quiet audio bugs that showed up as clicks and stalls: metering state under load, ARM64 denormals, send gain smoothing, and an audition deadlock.
- **fix**: Autosave writes atomically, so a crash mid-save leaves your last good project intact rather than half a file.
- **ci**: 13 test files that were never actually running are now wired into the build, plus a delay-line buffer fix they immediately caught.
