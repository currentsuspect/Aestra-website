v0.7.1-alpha is the trust sprint: exactly one new capability by design, and everything else removes a reason a first session ends badly. The week before it produced nine founder-reported bugs in one family — the UI told you one thing and the engine did another — and this release closes that seam: pattern edits reach playback, stop lands at zero, and recorded takes line up under latency.

- **new**: Fit an audio clip to N bars with varispeed tempo-fit — pitch follows tempo, no new DSP, no format change.
- **new**: Aestra Transient, an internal envelope shaper for drums: bipolar Attack/Sustain knobs around a centre detent with a live envelope sketch, zero latency.
- **new**: The mixer's plugin dropdown now shows every effect you have — Transient, the new internals, every VST3/CLAP — from a metadata catalog, opening where you clicked.
- **new**: Your timeline overview is back above the ruler — cropped to your tracks — and clips now snap onto lines you can actually see.
- **new**: Drag a box around any group of patterns or audio clips on the timeline and they're all selected — delete, duplicate and friends act on the whole bunch.
- **new**: You can now click anywhere on the Arsenal progress bar to jump straight to that spot — playing or stopped.
- **new**: Ctrl+Z now works on Arsenal step edits — painting, erasing, moving and velocity tweaks can all be undone and redone — and you can select steps and move, duplicate, or delete them with the keyboard.
- **new**: Takes live in lanes that belong to your track, deletable with full undo; track headers show lane counts; arming happens on the track header while the mixer's record button is now an input-monitor toggle; and the transport warns you when you try to record with nothing armed.
- **new**: Opening a project with moved audio files now tells you which files are missing and lets you relink them.
- **new**: Count-in now actually counts you in before recording, and discarded takes stop piling up on disk.
- **new**: Mixer Trim now remembers where you left it after save and reopen (pan and width are covered too), and duplicated patterns play right away instead of waiting for the loop.
- **new**: Right-click a clip to delete it instantly, and drag a sample straight into an empty Arsenal to start working.
- **fix**: Splitting clips or muting/unmuting lanes while playing now updates what you hear right away — split, delete, mute, solo, undo and drop all reschedule the pattern engine immediately.
- **fix**: Pressing stop once now returns the playhead to the top.
- **fix**: Recorded takes now land on the grid instead of late by your interface's latency.
- **fix**: Clips dropped into the timeline now show their waveform right away instead of staying blank until you touch something else.
- **fix**: Recording inside a project that already has clips and patterns no longer silently produces an empty take when a loop region is active.
- **fix**: Dragging a selection box on the timeline no longer fights your hand or freezes the mouse, and multi-select dragging no longer risks freezing the pointer.
- **fix**: Panning notes from the piano roll side panel now sticks immediately, and the piano-roll playhead now looks like the one in the timeline.
- **fix**: Deleting a step while the loop plays now silences it immediately, and placing steps no longer makes them sound before the playhead reaches them.
- **fix**: Record-arm now works on a fresh project out of the box.
- **fix**: Toggling input monitoring on a mixer channel now counts as a project change — undo works and the state survives closing the project.
- **fix**: Channel faders and pans now actually change the sound — no more moving a slider and hearing nothing.
- **fix**: Pause in the Arsenal no longer rewinds you — play picks up from where the playhead was.
- **fix**: Sliced patterns no longer show ghost notes or replay from the original start — each half contains exactly what plays there.
- **fix**: Recovery no longer doubles your tracks, and projects saved before the demo-automation cleanup no longer carry ghost automation on channel 1.
- **fix**: The piano-roll grid is quieter under your notes, the browser search field is taller with an icon, and the sequencer panel sizes itself to its content.
- **perf**: The red live-input waveform while recording now costs almost nothing to draw — long recording sessions stop cooking the render side.
