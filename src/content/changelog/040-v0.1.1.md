ASIO support and the first real pass at keeping the engine light.

- **new**: ASIO driver support on Windows, with a fallback path so a missing driver doesn't leave you with no sound and no explanation.
- **new**: Real-time waveform scrubbing — drag the playhead and hear the material under it.
- **perf**: Pan law and interpolation tables precomputed, cutting per-block cost so more tracks fit before the meter climbs.
- **fix**: Fixed a routing bug that could silence the master output after rebuilding the signal graph.
