v0.8.2-alpha makes third-party plugins usable in earnest, adds FLAC and MP3 export, teaches Aestra to update itself, and fixes a long list of things that got in the way of making music. 106 merges since v0.8.1-alpha.

- **shot**: /changelog/v0.8.2-alpha/export.png | The new Export window: what, where and the format, with the length and size before you export.
- **shot**: /changelog/v0.8.2-alpha/settings.png | Settings, rebuilt: one form on every page, and each change says what it does.
- **shot**: /changelog/v0.8.2-alpha/timeline.png | The timeline: track-colour clips, the toolbar row, and untouched tracks folded into one row.
- **shot**: /changelog/v0.8.2-alpha/updates.png | Settings > Updates: Aestra checks for, downloads and installs signed updates.
- **new**: VST3 and CLAP plugins open their own windows, running safely in the plugin sandbox, on Linux and Windows.
- **new**: Plugins know the song: tempo, time signature, play and stop, and the playhead, so synced delays and arpeggiators follow along.
- **new**: Export your song as FLAC or MP3 as well as WAV, from a simpler Export window that shows the length and size first.
- **new**: Aestra updates itself: it checks for a signed update, downloads it, and installs it when you restart.
- **new**: Save a copy of your project under a new name and keep working on the original.
- **new**: Hear a sample or an audio clip from inside its editor with Preview.
- **new**: In the piano roll, drag a stretch of the ruler and press Ctrl+D to copy it into the next stretch, again and again.
- **new**: Right-click a pattern in the Arsenal to rename, duplicate or delete it.
- **new**: A timeline toolbar, clips in their track's colour, automation lanes under their own track, and untouched tracks folded into one row.
- **new**: Sidechain Aestra's compressor from a sidechain send.
- **new**: The DSP load sits beside OUTPUT, amber when your computer is busy and red before the audio would drop out.
- **new**: Plug in a MIDI keyboard while Aestra is open and play it straight away.
- **new**: Every plugin Aestra makes is free, Rumble included, with no account needed.
- **fix**: Exporting right after playing a pattern in the Arsenal renders the song, not silence.
- **fix**: Sampler settings like mono, glide and voices come back exactly as saved when you reopen a project.
- **fix**: Projects from older versions reopen with each track on its own mixer channel instead of everything on the master.
- **fix**: The Arsenal and piano roll open a pattern with notes, not an empty Pattern 1.
- **fix**: Plugins in the sandbox no longer drop out, and a plugin that falls behind snaps back into time.
- **fix**: Back-to-back 808s no longer tick, EQ band changes no longer click, and the reverb tail is smooth.
- **fix**: Unplugging an audio interface, or switching off the output while playing, no longer needs a restart or freezes Aestra.
- **fix**: Stopping no longer leaves plugin notes hanging, and a tempo change keeps you on the same bar.
- **perf**: The interface redraws only when something changed, and does about a third less work when it does.
- **docs**: Moved to v0.8.3: the new piano roll, presets, in-app documentation, the second half of Audition and comparing a version with your current mix. Plugin windows and updates on Windows and macOS are tested in CI, not yet on a real machine of each.
