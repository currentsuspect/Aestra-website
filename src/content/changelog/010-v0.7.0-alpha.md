v0.7.0-alpha is the coherence milestone: routing and gain staging are correct end to end, automation sticks to the plugin it was drawn for, the Master channel hosts real effects, and the piano roll grew from an editor into a workflow. Three months of work — the engine got quieter and more predictable, and the UI stopped fighting you.

- **new**: One-shot samples now stop when their Piano Roll note ends instead of playing to the end of the sample.
- **new**: Now you can split an audio clip without the waveform restarting or showing a gap, and pitch it independently from speed.
- **new**: You can now split a selected Piano Roll note into snap-sized pieces.
- **new**: You can now stretch a selected Piano Roll phrase while preserving its internal timing.
- **new**: Sampler ping-pong loops now play backward and forward and reopen in the selected mode.
- **new**: You can now Shift-drag to paint chords in the Piano Roll and undo a whole drag erase at once.
- **new**: Piano Roll harmony choices now stay with the pattern when you reopen it.
- **new**: Routing through more mixer channels no longer lowers the sound, and Arsenal one-shots now keep their intended pitch and full tail.
- **new**: Aestra EQ now opens on a clean canvas, lets you delete every band, and uses a clearer response display.
- **new**: You can now pitch audio clips up or down by two octaves, with timeline waveforms that stay aligned to what you hear.
- **new**: On Windows, a crashing plugin no longer takes Aestra down with it.
- **new**: Timeline clips are easier on the eyes over a long session, and the minimap's colours now match the tracks they represent.
- **new**: The timeline is easier to read at a glance — clips stand out, the grid stays out of the way, and the minimap now clearly shows where you are in the song.
- **new**: Now Muse can inspect audio health, project-load integrity, and routing topology directly from the current Aestra session.
- **new**: Aestra now renders its intended crisp text even when launched outside the repository or build directory.
- **new**: Now you can edit audio clips with safer default headroom and route their shared source directly to any Mixer Insert.
- **new**: Now you can arrange clips freely and route their sources to any Mixer Insert from a searchable audio clip editor.
- **fix**: The Piano Roll view no longer follows the playhead while you edit unless you turn Follow on, and the reverb's mode selector is now a simple `‹ ›` navigator in the bottom deck.
- **fix**: Soloing a track now silences clips routed straight to the Master, matching what an isolated bounce of that track delivers.
- **fix**: Routing audio through a mixer channel no longer makes it quieter than routing it straight to the Master. You can now add effects to the Master channel like any other channel. Automation lanes are automatable again, and leftover test automation no longer rides along in new projects. The Audio Clip editor and Arsenal keep their layout when resized, and removing an effect and adding it again no longer leaves the insert area stuck.
- **fix**: Routing changes are now undoable — Ctrl+Z works on reroutes and sends, and routing loops are blocked instead of silently breaking audio.
- **fix**: Solo-bouncing a track now includes its sends' return path exactly as in the full mix.
- **fix**: Solo-bouncing a clip with Speed set now renders at the speed you hear live.
- **fix**: Deleting clips no longer risks crashing Aestra during timeline refreshes.
- **fix**: Now you can trim clips and have those edits survive saving and reopening a project.
- **fix**: Dragging timeline clips no longer jumps because of mixed coordinate origins.
- **fix**: Timeline selection, context menus, looping, and recovery dialogs no longer lose or misroute your input.
- **fix**: Select menus no longer hide reachable options or require two clicks, and tooltips no longer flicker, erase each other, or run off-screen.
