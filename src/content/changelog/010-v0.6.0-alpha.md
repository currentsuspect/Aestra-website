26 PRs. The unglamorous release — the one that makes a long session survivable.

- **security**: Full security audit — 11 findings, 8 fixed. Opening a project, recovering from a crash, and unpacking an archive are all hardened against malformed files.
- **security**: A scanned plugin can no longer impersonate a built-in one, and take snapshots stay inside your project folder where they belong.
- **new**: The engine now reads its state through a triple-buffered handoff, so the audio thread never waits on the interface — fewer clicks and dropouts when the session gets busy.
- **new**: A crashing VST3 gets contained instead of taking your session down, and any plugin spitting out garbage audio is quarantined and reported.
- **fix**: Waveform previews stop firing after you've moved on, and the audition decoder cleans up after itself instead of piling up.
- **fix**: Patterns come back the way you left them, and a tempo change now reaches the playlist, timeline, and pattern playback together.
- **perf**: The master safety limiter was reshaped to a transparent curve — it catches peaks without you hearing it work.
- **ci**: Thread-safety and leak detection added to the nightly build so callback regressions get caught before you do.
