v0.8.1-alpha rebuilds the places you spend the most time in. The Arsenal is a rack you play, the mixer reads meters first, the routing graph shows only what you changed, Audition is a place to listen and compare, and Versions replaces the history list. 88 pull requests since v0.8.0-alpha.

- **new**: The Arsenal is a rack you play. Sounds sit in rows with their output, patterns are one-click scenes under the header, and a row opens into step lanes.
- **new**: Start with a kit. An empty Arsenal offers to load a folder of sounds, and every file becomes a row named after it.
- **new**: Try sounds without stopping. A row's arrows step through its folder, and the swap lands on the next hit with no gap.
- **new**: Swing, chance and ratchets on every step. Chance plays a step only some of the time, a ratchet turns it into a quick roll, and swing pushes the off-beats.
- **new**: Place in timeline puts a pattern in your song. Press it again and the next copy follows the last one.
- **new**: The mixer reads meters first. Strips are grouped by where they send, with three sizes and a navigator for big sessions.
- **new**: The master shows loudness: integrated, short-term, true peak and stereo correlation.
- **new**: The routing graph shows only the routes you made. Select a channel to trace its chain, drag to patch, and a loop is refused.
- **new**: Audition is a place to listen. Play your mix against a reference at the same position and level, and switch between them without a cut.
- **new**: Versions replaces the History and Takes lists. Saved versions sit on a timeline you can branch from, bounce and play, or pull tracks and patterns from.
- **new**: A new library browser with Sounds, Patterns and Plugins tabs, one Places button for all your folders, and Add to Arsenal from the preview.
- **new**: One playhead everywhere, the same solid line in the timeline, piano roll and Arsenal.
- **fix**: Drum hits no longer drop when the screen is busy. Hits are now scheduled a quarter second ahead, and edits are still heard at once.
- **fix**: Opening a project plays it at its own tempo. It used to play at 120 while showing yours.
- **fix**: New Project and Open Project ask about unsaved work, as Quit does.
- **fix**: Trims and undo rebuild playback, so trimmed-off tails no longer play.
- **fix**: Closing a title-bar menu no longer crashes.
- **fix**: Audio devices are found by name, not by their place in the list.
- **fix**: Bounce in Place works on pattern clips.
- **fix**: The startup log names the real version, so a crash report says which build it came from.
- **docs**: Known gaps, stated plainly. Presets, in-app docs, the piano roll redesign, the second half of Audition and comparing a version with your current mix move to v0.8.2. Bouncing a version blocks the window while it renders, about three times faster than real time. Bounce in Place on a pattern used on several tracks ignores sends and group buses. The Arsenal and panels were checked on a 1366x768 laptop; the rest on a virtual display and in tests.
